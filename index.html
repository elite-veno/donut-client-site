Finders

Find the bases other players can't.

Every finder reads the chunks your client already holds, draws boxes and tracers to what it finds, and lists the nearest find with its distance and an arrow in the info panel.

BaseFinder

Scores each chunk on what worldgen never places: shulker boxes, beacons, ender chests, signs, redstone, concrete, rooms full of chests. Villages, ancient cities and trial chambers stay below the bar, so a mark like Base? 10: shulker box, 2 signs means a player built there.

DebrisFinder

Reads ancient debris straight from loaded chunks, sorts out anti-xray fakes, and lights up real debris with a sound the moment mining reveals it.

StorageFinder

Chests, trapped and ender chests, shulker boxes, barrels, hoppers, furnaces and storage minecarts.

SpawnerFinder

An untouched spawner's timer never moves, so it marks only the spawners a player has already been near.

TunnelFinder

Straight 1x2 tunnels, 1x1 holes and staircases that players dug. Anti-xray can't hide air, so it works on any server.

EntityFinder

Outlines players with their names through walls, and optionally mobs and dropped items.

ActivityFinder

Amethyst only grows while a player is nearby, so a grown geode means someone spent hours close to it.

Auto RTP

Teleports, lets ActivityFinder scan, and stops with a sound once an area shows real activity.

Trading bot

GoNuts trades the auction house for you.

GoNuts is built in. It learns prices from completed sales instead of asking prices, hunts flips, buys, relists, and books a sale only once it can prove it happened.

Off until you say so. GoNuts starts in observe mode and never trades until you turn live trading on and start a session. Settings that move money are marked in amber and need a second click.

Prices from real sales

Recency-weighted prices with outlier filtering and manipulation checks, so a rigged listing doesn't fool it.

Both sides of the market

Ranks resale flips across dozens of markets and reads the order house too.

Safety gates

Spend caps, dry run, wind down (sell what it holds, then stop) and an emergency stop on End.

Loadouts in one click

Apply a tested set of settings, like the recommended Veno loadout, and see exactly how many changed.

Web panel for your phone

Start, stop and watch your profit from a browser on your own Wi-Fi, behind a PIN.

FAQ

Questions

How long does a key last?

Two months from the day you buy it. It doesn't renew by itself: when it runs out, buy a new key to keep going.

How do I enter my key?

The first time you start Minecraft with Donut Client, it opens its Key page by itself. Paste the key from your email and press Enter. After that, the key is checked at every start, and it keeps working for 3 days without internet.

Can I get banned?

Yes, it's possible. DonutSMP's rules ban scripts and macros, and a ban can reach every account linked to a player. GoNuts stays off until you start it and has spend caps and an emergency stop, but using the client and the bot is a risk you choose to take.

Do I need GoNuts separately?

No, GoNuts is already inside Donut Client. Remove any separate GoNuts (doughbay) jar from your mods folder, or Fabric won't start.

Does it work with Sodium?

X-ray changes how chunks are drawn and needs Minecraft's own renderer, so it doesn't work with Sodium.

Can the finders see everything on the server?

They see what the server sends to your client: loaded chunks around you and the entities in them. Nothing reaches past that.

Which Minecraft versions are supported?

Minecraft Java 26.2 with Fabric.
